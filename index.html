Comparative Law Quick Guide

Key Differences: Doing Business in US vs UK

A quick reference matrix designed for founders and legal officers expanding their operations transatlantically.

Legal Metric 🇺🇸 United States (Delaware / Federal) 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (England & Wales)
Primary Business Entity Limited Liability Company (LLC) or C-Corporation Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd)
Corporate Privacy & Disclosures High privacy in Delaware / Wyoming (BOI reporting applies) High public transparency via Companies House (PSC register)
Consumer Privacy Framework Fragmented: CCPA (California), VCDPA, CPA, plus Sectoral laws Unified: UK General Data Protection Regulation & Data Protection Act
Employment Dismissal Rule Default "At-Will" (can terminate without reason unless illegal) Requires fair reason, consultation, and statutory notice
Non-Compete Enforceability Heavily restricted/scrutinized (e.g. California void, FTC rules) Permissible only if strictly limited in scope, duration, and geography
Contract Law Principle Freedom of contract, parole evidence rule, punitive damages in tort Strict reliance on written terms; no punitive damages for breach
