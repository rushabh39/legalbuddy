Our Mission

The Transatlantic Legal Intelligence Bridge

Founded to solve one pervasive issue: traditional legal advice is strictly localized, but modern business is borderless between the United States and the United Kingdom.

Why LegalBuddy Exists

Why Cross-Border Law Demands a New Approach

An American company launching an AI app or digital service will instantly encounter UK data subjects governed by the UK GDPR. Similarly, a London-based digital agency signing contracts with corporate clients in New York or Delaware must navigate US governing law, indemnification standards, and sales tax nexus.

Most legal blogs focus solely on either US law (Delaware/California) or UK law (England & Wales). LegalBuddy is built specifically to compare, contrast, and synthesize both legal universes into pragmatic, battle-tested decision frameworks.
