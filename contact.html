Frequently Asked Questions

Can LegalBuddy act as my formal counsel?

No. LegalBuddy is an independent educational publication and legal research organization. While our contributors are qualified attorneys and solicitors, we do not enter into formal attorney-client engagements through this website.

Can I pitch an article idea or guest piece?

Yes! We welcome submissions from practicing US attorneys, UK solicitors/barristers, and academic legal scholars. Select "Editorial Contribution" in the form opposite.

How frequently are legal guides updated?

Our editorial team reviews statutory updates, appellate decisions, and regulatory enforcement actions quarterly to ensure references to UK GDPR, Delaware Chancery law, and US federal regulations remain accurate.
