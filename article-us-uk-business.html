Starting a Business in the US vs. UK: Delaware LLC vs. UK Ltd Company Formation & Liabilities

Expanding your digital agency, consulting firm, or startup across the pond? Deciding between an American Delaware LLC and an English Private Limited Company (Ltd) involves fundamental differences in pass-through taxation, corporate veil protection, and statutory transparency.

H
Harrison Reed, Esq.
Cross-Border Corporate Attorney, New York Bar
Published: November 02, 2026 • Editorial Review

💡 Critical Distinction for International Founders

In the United States, an LLC is celebrated for its pass-through tax treatment (profits and losses flow directly to owners' personal tax returns). However, if an English resident operates a US LLC, the UK HMRC does not automatically treat a Delaware LLC as a pass-through entity; HMRC may classify it as an "opaque" corporate entity, creating potential double-taxation traps under the UK-US Double Tax Treaty unless structured precisely.

1. Entity Structure & Legal Identity

When international founders decide where to incorporate their enterprise, the two most popular corporate vehicles are:

  • The Delaware Limited Liability Company (US LLC): Governed by the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (Title 6, Chapter 18). It is celebrated globally for maximum contractual freedom. The owners ("Members") write an Operating Agreement that dictates profit shares, voting power, and management duties without rigid statutory officer mandates.
  • The UK Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd): Governed by the UK Companies Act 2006. It is an independent legal person with issued shares, statutory directors, and standardized Articles of Association. All official changes are submitted to Companies House in Cardiff/London.

2. Direct Side-by-Side Corporate Comparison

Category 🇺🇸 US Delaware LLC 🇬🇧 UK Private Limited Company (Ltd)
Formation Speed & Cost \$90 - \$300 state filing fee; 24-48 hours with registered agent. £50 filing fee directly with Companies House; 24 hours.
Public Register / Privacy Delaware does not publish member or manager names publicly. (FinCEN BOI filed confidentially). Companies House public register shows Director names, service address, and Persons with Significant Control (PSC).
Default Taxation Pass-through partnership (or disregarded entity for single-member LLCs). No Delaware corporate income tax if not doing business physically in DE. Subject to UK Corporation Tax (19% small profits rate, 25% main rate). Dividends taxed at shareholder level.
Annual Maintenance \$300 flat annual Delaware franchise tax + registered agent fee (~ \$100/yr). Annual Confirmation Statement (£34) + Statutory Annual Accounts filed with Companies House.
Piercing Corporate Veil Extremely rare in Delaware courts; strict contractual sanctity and Chancery Court business expertise. High protection under Salomon v Salomon; courts rarely pierce unless fraudulent concealment is proven.

3. Raising Venture Capital & Investment Considerations

If your goal is to raise institutional venture capital from American funds (Silicon Valley, NYC, Boston) vs. European/UK angels and funds (London, Cambridge):

  • US VC Requirement: US institutional venture funds almost never invest directly into an LLC. If you plan to issue equity grants (options, RSUs) or take Series Seed/A funding, you will need a Delaware C-Corporation rather than an LLC. However, an LLC can be easily converted into a C-Corp via statutory merger later.
  • UK Seed Enterprise Investment Scheme (SEIS / EIS): The UK offers extraordinary tax relief to angel investors (up to 50% income tax relief + capital gains exemption). To qualify for SEIS/EIS, the business must be a UK company with a permanent establishment in the UK.

4. Transatlantic Banking & Merchant Accounts

One of the greatest operational friction points is securing business bank accounts:

  1. US Banking: Obtaining an Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS for foreign founders without a Social Security Number (SSN) requires Form SS-4 filing via fax/mail, which can take 3 to 6 weeks. Modern fintech providers (Mercury, Relay) allow remote onboarding once the EIN is issued.
  2. UK Banking: UK high-street banks (Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds) enforce strict anti-money laundering (AML) checks and often require at least one UK-resident director to open a traditional business checking account. Non-resident founders frequently turn to digital banks such as Wise Business or Revolut Business.

5. Conclusion: Which Should You Choose?

Choose a Delaware LLC if you are targeting predominantly US consumers or clients, wish to minimize public disclosure of ownership details, and operate a lean digital service or e-commerce store.

Choose a UK Ltd Company if you have British founders or UK resident staff, wish to benefit from lower incorporation maintenance costs, want to capture the UK market, or intend to leverage SEIS/EIS angel fundraising.

Cross-Border Tax Advisory Notice

Always consult both a US Certified Public Accountant (CPA) and a UK Chartered Tax Advisor (CTA) prior to executing transatlantic legal formation to avoid unintentionally creating a Permanent Establishment (PE) or conflicting corporate tax residency under the US-UK Double Tax Treaty.
